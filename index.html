A little about me

Make it
mean it.

I'm Diana, a designer and creative developer based in Mexico. I build brands, identities, and digital products from first sketch to final form.

My work moves between visual identity, UI/UX, textile design, and product storytelling. Projects have been shown at Milan Design Week, and I've built visual systems for teams in biotech, fashion-tech, and sustainability. I like a good idea best when it works in real life, too.

Always collecting references, making things, and looking for the next good question.
